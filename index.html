Questions worth asking before hydro jetting.

Is hydro jetting suitable for every pipe?

No. Ask a qualified professional to assess the line, access and blockage before recommending high-pressure cleaning.

Can cleaning repair a broken sewer pipe?

Cleaning removes an obstruction; it does not rebuild a damaged pipe. Ask whether the inspection shows a repair issue as well as a blockage.

What details should I provide with a request?

List the affected fixtures, whether wastewater has backed up and any previous cleaning. Mention known pipe repairs or access limitations.

Is service availability confirmed for my address?

Availability must be confirmed for the address and work requested. A request is not a booked appointment or a guarantee of service.
